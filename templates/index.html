The Science

How LumiPix Restores Your Images

Every algorithm is carefully implemented using proven signal-processing mathematics.

Smart Adaptive Deblur LumiPix Core

An 8-step automatic pipeline. First, it decomposes your image into luminance + color in LAB space. Then applies Richardson–Lucy iterative deconvolution with an auto-estimated PSF. Multi-scale detail recovery layers at 3 different frequencies (fine, medium, coarse). CLAHE normalization reveals hidden tones. Total Variation denoising removes ringing. Finally, Guided Filter edge-polishing and chroma boost produce sharp, vivid, natural-looking results.

In+1 = In · (hT * (Y / (h * In))) Richardson–Lucy Update

Wiener Deconvolution

Inverts the blur in the frequency domain using a known PSF. Balances sharpness vs. noise amplification via the regularization constant K.

W(f) = H*(f) / (|H(f)|² + K) Wiener Filter Transfer Function

Unsharp Masking

Subtracts a blurred copy from the original to extract a high-pass "mask" of edges, then adds it back amplified.

Isharp = I + α(I − Gσ*I) Unsharp Mask Formula
